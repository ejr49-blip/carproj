Sports Car Evolution

A curated exhibition tracing design, engineering, and emotion.

Enter the Exhibition

Narrative Flow

From early experiments in lightness to the golden age of analog performance, through high-performance engineering and electrification — this exhibition maps how sports cars evolved as technology and culture collided.

  • Origins: lightweight experiments & bespoke coachwork.
  • Golden Era: styling became identity and emotion.
  • Modernization: aerodynamics, materials, and electronics.
  • Today: hybrid, electric, and the future of performance.
